MD|Hardware  PERFORM · M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite

One layout for MD442, MD187 and MD888. PATCH is SHIFT: hold it for every PATCH + combo. The 10 end keys of the keybed are your build-up and breakdown tools: hold to play, let go to come back. Version 1.4, not yet tested on a keyboard.

Why it's laid out this way

One thing selected, every control on it (Roland TR-8S, Ableton Push): pick an instrument and the pads, K2-K4 and the mod wheel all follow it, so you never hunt for a page.

Mutes and solo one touch away (Elektron mute mode, Maschine SHIFT solo): KNOB BANK turns the pads into mutes, PATCH + a pad solos.

Moves you hold, not settings you change (TR-8S scatter, Maschine Perform FX, a DJ mixer's filter and echo): the end keys play while held and let go cleanly. Two hands, no shift.

Everything lands on the one: patterns change at the bar, and KILL LOW, RISER and DROP come back on the next downbeat, so a drop can't be late.

Build a beat

Pick the instrumentA black key, or OCT- OCT+ to flick BD, SD ... 303A, 303B, BREAK. PATCH + OCT: the part
StepsPads (or the white keys) are its 16 steps: tap on, tap off, hold for that step's sound. < >: bars 1-4
Play it inSEQ REC + play keys or pads. PATCH + FADER BANK: DRUM PADS, velocity, records to the nearest step
Shape itK2 filter (left low-pass, right high-pass, rests at OFF), K3 resonance, K4 decay. 303: cutoff, reso, env mod. BREAK: pitch

Mix it live

LevelsFaders A: 909, 808, 303A, 303B. FADER BANK: break, reverb, delay, master filter
MuteTap KNOB BANK (lit), pads 1-11 mute tracks, 12-16 parts. Hold it instead: mutes while held
SoloIn MUTE: PATCH + a pad. Again to unsolo. PATCH + KNOB BANK: unmute all
Next scenePATCH + < >, or PATCH + pad 1-16. Changes at the end of the bar

Build-ups and breakdowns: the end keys (hold)

C3 C#3 D3 D#3BEAT REPEAT 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 bar. The fastest held wins: roll your hand right to tighten it. Let go: back in time
E3 FILLA roll on the selected drum (the snare if none): 8ths, 16ths from the next bar, then 32nds, getting louder
G#5 ECHO THROWSends the part on screen into the delay. Let go: the echoes ring out for a bar
A5 KILL LOWKicks and both 303s out. Back on the next one
A#5 WASHReverb on the part on screen. PATCH + ECHO or WASH: every part
B5 RISERMaster high-pass sweeps up over 4 bars, reverb swells. Back on the next one
C6 DROPEverything out, the reverb and echo tails ring. Back on the next one
Classic build: hold RISER + FILL for 2-4 bars, add DROP on the last beat, let everything go together. It all lands on the one.

Everything else

SEQ PLAY SEQ RECPlay / stop; record (knob moves too). PATCH + K2/K3/K4: tempo / part / pattern
Other buttonsFX, SCALE, CHORD, NOTE RPT, PARA, GLOBE, BT, ARP, SEQ: as in MD|442 1.2 (FX, list, accent, slide, sound, settings, save, TB-3PO, steps)
In a list, a question or GLOBE's settings, K2-K4 are SELECT, ALGORITHM and PRESETS again. Filters start at OFF on power-up. Update mode: hold < and > for 5 s.
