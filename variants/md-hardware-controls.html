MD|Hardware  PERFORM controls · M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite

One layout for all three firmwares: MD442, MD187 and MD888. PATCH is SHIFT: hold it for the PATCH + combos. Version 1.3, not yet tested on a keyboard.

Play a set

1. Pick a patternPATCH + pad 1-16, or PATCH + < >. It changes at the end of the bar.
2. Pick an instrumentOCT- OCT+ flick through BD, SD, CP, CH, OH ... 303A, 303B, BREAK. The screen names it.
3. Shape itK2 filter (turn left: low-pass, right: high-pass, it stops at OFF in the middle), K3 resonance, K4 tone.
4. Drop and bring backTap KNOB BANK, hit pads to mute, tap again to go back to steps. Or hold it and hit pads.
5. Build upPush the mod wheel up: a high-pass riser on the instrument. Let go to drop.

The three firmwares

MD442Techno and house, 142 BPM. Your 7 MD|442 beats on patterns 1-7. Keeps your saved patterns.
MD18790s hip-hop, 90 BPM, swung. Boom-bap break, dusty sampled kit, Rhodes / horn / string stabs, vocal hits and a scratch.
MD888Hard techno, 155 BPM. Driven kick, rumble, gabba kicks, supersaw trance stabs, industrial hits, acid.
Switch firmware with Install MD Hardware (Mac).command: pick 1-3 for the firmware, then 1 for the Elite. All MD187 / MD888 samples are synthesised from scratch. GPL-3.0, built on X0X.

Every control

OCT- / OCT+Previous / next instrument: the 909's 11 tracks, the 808's 11, 303A, 303B, BREAK
PATCH + OCT-/OCT+Previous / next part (909, 808, 303A, 303B, BREAK)
< / >The bars: steps 1-16, 17-32, 33-48, 49-64. On a 303 keyboard (REC on): its octave. On TB-3PO: mutate / new line
PATCH + </>Previous / next pattern, all parts, at the end of the bar
K2 K3 K4The selected instrument's FILTER, RESONANCE and TONE. Drums: tone = decay. 303: cutoff, reso, env mod. BREAK: filter, reso, pitch
PATCH + K2/K3/K4Tempo / part / pattern
KNOB BANKTap: MUTE pads on (lit) or off. Hold: mute while held
PATCH + KNOB BANKUnmute everything
PadsThe selected instrument's steps (1-8 top row). PATCH + pad: pattern
Black keys (keybed)Pick a drum track. With the BREAK selected: play its 8 slices live (stabs, vocal hits)
Mod wheel / pitch wheelDrum or BREAK: high-pass riser. 303: opens its cutoff / bends its tune
K1, K5-K8Volume; the four knobs on screen (page values)
FadersBank A: 909, 808, 303A, 303B levels. FADER BANK: break, reverb, delay, master filter
SEQ PLAY SEQ RECPlay / stop; record (knob moves too). PATCH + REC: undo
FX, SCALE, CHORD, NOTE RPT, PARA, GLOBE, BT, ARP, SEQAs before: FX, list, accent, mixer, sound, settings, save, TB-3PO, steps
In a list, a question or GLOBE's settings, K2-K4 go back to SELECT, ALGORITHM and PRESETS. The filters start at OFF each time the keyboard starts. Update mode: hold < and > together for 5 seconds.
