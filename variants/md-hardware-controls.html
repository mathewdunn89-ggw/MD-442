MD|Hardware 1.6  ·  M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite

One layout for MD442, MD187 and MD888 (also on the SMK-37 Pro). The keys play. The pads are the steps. < > picks the instrument. FX switches what the faders do. Your global settings (MIDI, clock, output, keys, autosave) are never changed. Version 1.6 has not been tested on a keyboard yet.

1Pick an instrument: < >

DRUMS 1909 kit
DRUMS 2808 kit
BASS303 A
CHORDSnew
BREAKSloops
The screen shows its name. The keys, the pads, the sound knobs and the mod wheel all follow it.

2The keys play it (always)

DRUMS 1 / 2From F3 up: BD SD CP CH OH LT MT HT RS CR RD, then the same again from E4. Hit harder for louder
BASSNotes. Hold one key and play another: it slides. OCT- OCT+: octave
CHORDSOne key = a whole chord (a minor chord to start), an octave above the bass
BREAKSSlices 1-8 of the loop, from F3 up
AccentHit hard, or hold CHORD while you play
The keys never turn steps on or off. The two end zones are the performance keys (card 7).

3The pads are the steps

Tap a padThat step on. Tap again: off. Pads 1-16 = steps 1-16
DRUMSThe pads show the drum you last played on the keys. Play another key to edit that drum
BASS / CHORDSA new step takes the note you last played. Hold a pad + press a key: that step's note
Bars 2-4OCT- OCT+ on the drums: steps 17-32 and on

4FX switches the faders

FX litREVERB · DELAY · DRIVE · FILTER (middle off, left low-pass, right high-pass). The screen shows the effects
FX offVolumes: DRUMS 1 · DRUMS 2 · BASS · CHORDS
FADER BANKFader 1 switches between DRUMS 1 and BREAKS (lit = BREAKS)
Hold a pad + any faderThat instrument's volume: pads 1-11 the drums of the kit on screen, 12-16 DRUMS 1, DRUMS 2, BASS, CHORDS, BREAKS (1.7)
A fader only takes over once you move it, so nothing jumps when you switch.

5Record

REC + PLAYPlay the keys in time: drums, bass and chords go into the pattern on the nearest step
Or by handStop, play a note, tap the pads where you want it
SaveBT saves (and it saves itself when it's quiet)

6Shape the sound

Hold KNOB BANKK2 filter, K3 resonance, K4 decay. BASS / CHORDS: cutoff, resonance, env mod
PARAThe instrument's sound pages (press again: next page). K5-K8 turn what's on screen
Chord typeOn CHORDS: PARA until the CHORD page, K5: Minor, Major, Min7, Maj7, Min9, Sus4, 5th, or Off
Mod wheelBASS / CHORDS: opens the filter. Drums / breaks: a high-pass riser

8Mutes and patterns

Tap KNOB BANKPads become mutes (lit): 1-11 the drums, 12-16 the five instruments. Tap again: steps. Hold instead: mutes while held
SoloIn mutes: PATCH + a pad. PATCH + KNOB BANK: unmute all
Next patternPATCH + < >, or PATCH + a pad. Changes at the end of the bar
