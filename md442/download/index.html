MD|442

A free drum machine, developed by Mathew. No sign-up, nothing to pay. Take it with you as an app on your Mac or PC, on your phone, or as firmware that runs on the keyboard itself.

  • 13 analogue-style drums and an accent, an acid bass and dub stab chords
  • 16 steps, 8 scenes that each keep their own mutes and mix, and chain mode
  • Mixer, reverb, dub echo, filter, drive, kills and hold repeat
  • Plays with your MIDI keyboard, Ableton and Maschine, and records WAV
  • Beats move between the browser, the apps and the DAW plugin
  • Free. No sign-up, no ads

Guides

Install the firmware

  1. Mac: download the installer pack and unzip it. Connect the keyboard with a USB data cable. Right-click "Install MD-442 (Mac).command", choose Open, and pick your keyboard (1 to 3). The first time, macOS may offer to install Python's command line tools: say yes, then run it again.
  2. Windows or Linux: unzip the installer pack, open a terminal in the folder, run pip install mido python-rtmidi, then python3 tools/fm1_install.py md442-smk37-elite.fwsc (use the file for your keyboard).
  3. The installer checks the file matches the connected keyboard before writing. When it says done, the keyboard restarts into MD|442.

Play the firmware

  • PLAY starts pattern 1, Peak Warehouse at 142 BPM.
  • Change pattern: hold HOME (PATCH on the SMK-37) and press pad or white key 1 to 7: Peak Warehouse, Berlin Roll, Electro, Acid Jack, Deep House, Breakbeat, Dub Techno.
  • Steps: the 16 pads on the SMK-37, the white keys on the FM-1.
  • Controls guide for both keyboards (PDF, 7 pages)

Play MD|442 (browser, phone, desktop)

  • ▶ PLAY starts the beat and the same key, now ■ STOP, stops it. Space bar does the same; so do your SMK-37 and a DAW sending MIDI start/stop.
  • Tap a drum, then tap its steps. Double-tap a drum (double-click on a computer) to mute or unmute it.
  • MUTE GROUP: hold it, tap the drums you want, press DONE. FLIP then mutes or unmutes them all together on the next bar.
  • Scenes keep their own pattern, mutes, filter, effects and mix. Tap + to save the next scene; CHAIN plays them in turn.
  • MY BEATS: SAVE keeps a beat on this device. EXPORT saves it as a .md442.json file, IMPORT opens one (from the plugin or another device), MIDI ↓ saves a MIDI clip for your DAW.
  • MIDI · DAW (on the phone: ☰ menu): CONNECT a keyboard, LEARN any knob or pad, CLOCK OUT so an FM-1 or other gear follows MD|442's tempo, SEND TO KEYBOARD for firmware 1.1.

Desktop app

  • Press the arrows button on the console to fill your screen, and ⌘K (Ctrl K on Windows) for every shortcut.
  • Plug in a MIDI controller before or after opening the app: pads play the drums, keys play the bass or the stab.
  • Mac: the first time you open it, macOS says it cannot check the app. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, and confirm.
  • Windows: if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Plug in an SMK-37 or FM-1 and press CONNECT to play the app from the keyboard. The app works offline.

Install the plugin

  1. Mac: open md442-plugin-mac.pkg. It isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time right-click it and choose Open, then Open (on macOS 15: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway).
  2. Windows: run md442-plugin-windows.exe. If SmartScreen warns, press More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Maschine 2: Preferences › Plug-ins › Manager › Rescan. Load MD-442 from Instruments › NEST Group.
  4. Ableton Live: Settings › Plug-Ins: turn on Use Audio Units (Mac) or Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders, then Rescan. MD-442 is under Plug-Ins › NEST Group.
  5. Logic Pro: instrument slot › AU Instruments › NEST Group › MD-442.

Play the plugin

  • The pattern plays with your DAW, at its tempo. Turn PATTERN off to play only from pads, keys or clips.
  • Notes C1–C2 (and C3–C4 for Maschine's keyboard mode): Kick, Snare, Clap, Rim, Low/Mid/Hi tom, Closed/Open hat, Crash, Ride, Bass, Stab. Hit hard for the accent.
  • MIDI channel 2 plays the bass at the note you play; channel 3 plays the dub stab chord.
  • Right-click any knob › MIDI learn, then turn a knob on your SMK-37 or other controller. Maschine's encoders get the knobs in pages of 8.
  • DRAG MIDI drops the beat onto a track as a MIDI clip. IMPORT / EXPORT move beats as .md442.json files. SEND TO KEYBOARD puts the beat on an SMK-37 / FM-1 with firmware 1.1 (patterns 9–16).
  • Beats go both ways: EXPORT in the plugin (it saves to Documents/MD-442/Beats), then IMPORT under MY BEATS in the browser, phone or desktop app, and the same the other way round. MIDI ↓ in the apps saves a MIDI clip too.

Before you flash

  • Firmware replaces the keyboard's own. You can always go back, but you do it at your own risk.
  • Keep the keyboard powered the whole time.
  • To go back, install M-VAVE's own package for your model with M-VAVE's updater (M-UPGRADE).
