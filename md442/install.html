MD-MODULES

Three drum machines for your keyboard: MD442, MD187 and MD888. Pick one and install it straight from Chrome on Mac or Windows. Nothing to download. How to play it is further down.

1Your keyboard

2Pick your MD-Module

The MD-Modules are three different drum machines, each with its own sounds, patterns and samples. They all play the same way. The keyboard holds one at a time, and you can swap whenever you like: pick another and install. Your settings are kept.

3Install

  1. Quit M-UPGRADE, the MD|442 app, other MD|442 tabs and any DAW. Only one app can talk to the keyboard at a time.
  2. Plug the keyboard straight into the computer with a USB data cable (no hub) and switch it on.
  3. Press the button below. When Chrome asks, choose Allow for MIDI.
  4. Keep the cable in until it says Done. It takes a few minutes, and the keyboard restarts by itself.

New in 1.7: hold a pad and move a fader for that instrument's volume, and two fixes. As in 1.6, the keys always play the instrument on screen, the pads are the steps, < and > pick the instrument (DRUMS 1, DRUMS 2, BASS, CHORDS, BREAKS), and FX switches the faders between effects and volumes. How to play it is further down this page (PDF). 1.7 has not been tested on a keyboard yet; 1.6 and 1.5 are below if you want one back.

HOW TO PLAY 1.7

The actual mapping of the MD-Modules (version 1.7) on the SMK-37 Elite and Pro. All three play the same way. One-page PDF to print.

The ideaKeysPadsButtonsKnobsFadersRecordBuild-upsMutes and patternsWith the appModule or M-VAVE

The idea in four lines

< >Pick the instrument. The screen shows its name.
KeysAlways play that instrument. They never turn steps on or off.
PadsAre its 16 steps. Tap a pad: step on. Tap again: off.
FXSwitches the faders. Lit: effects. Off: volumes.
DRUMS 1909 kit DRUMS 2808 kit BASS303 bass CHORDSone key, a chord BREAKSloops and hits

The five instruments, in the order < > steps through them. The keys, the pads, the sound knobs and the mod wheel all follow the one on screen.

Keys

On DRUMS 1 / DRUMS 2Each key is a drum. From F3 going up: kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, low tom, mid tom, high tom, rim, crash, ride (DRUMS 2: cymbal, cowbell). The same eleven repeat from E4. Hit harder for louder.
On BASSNotes. Hold one key and press another and it slides to it.
On CHORDSOne key plays a whole chord, an octave above the bass.
On BREAKSSlices 1 to 8 of the loop, from F3 up (MD187: stabs and vocal hits, MD888: trance stabs and hits).
OCT- OCT+On BASS and CHORDS: the keys' octave. On the drums and breaks: the next or previous 16 steps on the pads.
AccentHit the key hard, or hold CHORD while you play.
Pitch wheelBends BASS or CHORDS.
Mod wheelBASS / CHORDS: opens the filter. Drums / breaks: a high-pass riser on the one on screen.
The 5 keys at each endNot notes. They are the build-up keys, below.

Pads

Tap a padThat step on. Tap again: off. Pads 1 to 16 are steps 1 to 16 (top row 1 to 8).
DrumsThe pads show the drum you last played on the keys. Play another drum key to see and edit that drum's steps.
BASS / CHORDSA new step takes the note you last played. Hold a pad and press a key: that step gets that note.
Hold a pad + turn K5-K8On the PARA screen: change the sound for that step only.
Hold a pad + turn K2That step's chance of playing.
PATCH + a padGo to that pattern (1 to 16) at the end of the bar.
BT + a padPaste the copied pattern there (hold BT a moment first to copy the current one).
After a tap on KNOB BANKThe pads are mutes, below.

Buttons

PLAYStart and stop.
RECRecord on and off. With PLAY running, what you play on the keys goes into the pattern.
< >The instrument before and after. PATCH + them: the pattern before and after.
FXFaders are effects (lit) or volumes (off). Lit also shows the effects on screen.
FADER BANKWith FX off: fader 1 is DRUMS 1 (off) or BREAKS (lit).
KNOB BANKTap: the pads become mutes (lit). Hold: K2, K3, K4 shape the instrument.
PATCHThe shift button, and on its own the pattern screen (press again: the song).
PARAThe instrument's sound pages. Press again for the next page.
SEQThe instrument's sequencer page: length, speed, direction.
NOTE REPEATThe mixer screen: level, pan, reverb and delay for each part and each drum.
ARPOn BASS or CHORDS: the line generator. OCT+ writes a new line, OCT- changes it a little.
CHORDHold while playing: accent.
SCALEShows the screen as a list. In a question: yes.
GLOBESettings: MIDI, clock, output. Yours are kept through every update.
BTSave. It also saves by itself when things are quiet.
PATCH + REC / PLAYUndo / redo.
BT + RECClear the instrument on screen in this pattern (it asks first).
OCT- + OCT+ held 5 secondsUpdate mode, the same as stock. M-UPGRADE and the restore below can always reach the keyboard.

Knobs

K1Master volume.
K2Moves through the pages on screen, or up and down a list. PATCH + K2: tempo.
K3The instrument (the same as < >). In a list: the value.
K4The pattern, changing at the end of the bar.
K5-K8The four things shown on screen.
Hold KNOB BANK + K2 K3 K4Drums and breaks: filter (left low-pass, right high-pass, middle off), resonance, decay. BASS / CHORDS: cutoff, resonance, envelope.
Chord typeOn CHORDS press PARA until the page says CHORD, then turn K5: Minor, Major, Min7, Maj7, Min9, Sus4, 5th, or Off (a second bass).

Faders

FX lit1 REVERB · 2 DELAY · 3 DRIVE · 4 FILTER (middle off, down low-pass, up high-pass).
FX off1 DRUMS 1 · 2 DRUMS 2 · 3 BASS · 4 CHORDS volume.
FADER BANK litFader 1 is BREAKS volume instead of DRUMS 1.
Hold a pad + any faderThat pad's instrument volume. Pads 1 to 11: the drums of the kit on screen (kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, toms, rim, crash, ride). Pads 12 to 16: DRUMS 1, DRUMS 2, BASS, CHORDS, BREAKS. The step or mute the press changed is put back.

A fader only takes over once you move it, so nothing jumps when you switch.

Record a beat

  1. Pick the instrument with < >.
  2. Press REC, then PLAY.
  3. Play the keys in time. Drums, bass notes and chords land on the nearest step and loop.
  4. Fix it on the pads: tap a step off, tap a new one on.
  5. Next instrument, and do it again. BT saves.

Without REC: play a note, then tap the pads where you want it.

Build-ups and breakdowns: the 5 keys at each end (hold)

C3, C#3, D3, D#3Beat repeat: 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 bar. Let go and it is back in time.
E3 FILLA roll on the drum on screen, faster and louder each bar.
G#5 ECHOThrows the instrument into the delay. The echoes ring for a bar after you let go.
A5 KILL LOWKicks and bass out. Back on the next one.
A#5 WASHReverb on the instrument. PATCH + ECHO or WASH: on everything.
B5 RISERA high-pass sweep up over 4 bars. Back on the next one.
C6 DROPEverything out, tails ring. Back on the next one.

Mutes and patterns

Tap KNOB BANKPads become mutes (lit). Pads 1 to 11: the drums of the kit on screen. Pads 12 to 16: DRUMS 1, DRUMS 2, BASS, CHORDS, BREAKS. Tap it again: back to steps.
Hold KNOB BANK + padsMute only while you hold.
PATCH + a pad (in mutes)Solo it. Again: solo off.
PATCH + KNOB BANKUnmute everything.
PATCH + < >The pattern before or after, at the end of the bar.
PATCH + a padThat pattern. OCT+ on the pattern screen: patterns 17 to 32.

On its own, and with the app

On its ownNo computer needed. The sound comes out of the keyboard's own audio out. Everything above works the same.
Plugged into the MD|442 app (USB)One play button for both. PLAY or STOP on the keyboard starts and stops the app and sets its tempo. START in the app starts the keyboard in time. They never run two separate beats.
Send your beatsIn the app: MIDI · DAW, then SEND TO KEYBOARD. Your scenes 1 to 8 land on keyboard patterns 9 to 16.
With a DAW or other gearThe keyboard sends and follows MIDI clock, start and stop over USB. Change this under GLOBE.
Unplug itIt carries on by itself at its own tempo.

MD-Module or M-VAVE's own firmware?

The keyboard holds oneInstalling an MD-Module replaces M-VAVE's firmware. There is not enough memory in the keyboard for both, or for all three modules at once.
With an MD-ModuleThe keyboard is a drum machine that makes its own sound. No computer needed.
What you give up meanwhileM-VAVE's controller features: its own arp, chord, scale and note repeat modes, Bluetooth, and working as a plain MIDI controller for a DAW or for the MD|442 app's pads and keys.
SwappingA few minutes either way, as often as you like: another module at the top of this page, or M-VAVE's own firmware back. Your module settings are kept.

1.7 has not been tested on a real keyboard yet. If something here does not match what the keyboard does, the keyboard is wrong, not you: go back to 1.6 or 1.5 above and say what you pressed.
