MD|442

A free drum machine, developed by Mathew. Analogue-style drums, an acid bass, dub chords and a row of effects. Play it here for as long as you like, or download it.

Three machines in one: MD|442 techno · MD|187 90s hip-hop · MD|888 hard techno. Switch under the logo.

AndroidDownload the Android app (.apk), open it and tap Install. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser once.
iPhoneOpen the phone app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Works in Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
DesktopThe full console is on this page. Press the arrows button on the console to fill your screen, and ⌘K for every shortcut.
