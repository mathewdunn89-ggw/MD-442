# MD|187 and MD|888 sample loops

Free loops from MD-Modules. Ready-made packs with every loop also cut into its 8 slices:
[MD187-sample-pack.zip](https://github.com/mathewdunn89-ggw/MD-MODULE/releases/download/sample-packs/MD187-sample-pack.zip) ·
[MD888-sample-pack.zip](https://github.com/mathewdunn89-ggw/MD-MODULE/releases/download/sample-packs/MD888-sample-pack.zip)

| File | Style | BPM | Bars |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `md187/BOOMBP.wav` | Boom-bap drum loop | 96 | 1 |
| `md187/DRUMS.wav` | Dusty drum loop | 96 | 1 |
| `md187/STABS.wav` | Stabs | 96 | 1 |
| `md187/VOX.wav` | Vocal-style hits | 96 | 1 |
| `md888/RUMBLE.wav` | Rumble kick loop | 155 | 1 |
| `md888/GABBA.wav` | Gabba kick loop | 155 | 1 |
| `md888/TRANCE.wav` | Trance stabs | 155 | 2 |
| `md888/HITS.wav` | Hits | 155 | 1 |

WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono. Each loop cuts into 8 equal slices and every slice starts on a hit.

**Where they come from**: every sound is synthesised from scratch by [`tools/gen_banks.py`](../tools/gen_banks.py)
(oscillators, noise and filters). Nothing is recorded, and nothing is taken from a record or from another product.
Run `python3 tools/make_sample_packs.py` to rebuild these files and the packs.
