Biography

20 years behind the decks

It started in a friend's flat in Glasgow — two decks, a crate of vinyl, and Dave Clarke's World Service on repeat.

20 years on MATHEW has spent a career DJing and promoting club nights across Scotland, Berlin and Thailand. Bringing huge names like Steffi, Midland, George Fitzgerald, Nina Kraviz and DjRum to the dance floors in Scotland.

To this day his passion is driving house, dub and techno. Now a promoter and head honcho at NOSLEEP. CLAN on koh Phangan, it's guaranteed you will enjoy his unique underground style.
